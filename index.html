The Challenge: Post-COVID Academic Decline

Since remote learning during the pandemic, computer science and STEM departments have faced unprecedented loss of academic integrity and student passive disengagement.

  • Rampant copy-pasting from generic online answer repositories.
  • Students lack deep algorithmic intuition and execution optimization skills.
  • Professors are overwhelmed grading hundreds of uninspired homework submissions.
Impact: 68% of professors report declining foundational knowledge in upper-level courses.

The Solution: Daily Peer-Sparked Mastery

Daily Spark turns course engagement into a daily peer-driven challenge ecosystem modeled like StackOverflow, restricted exclusively to your university course program.

  • Daily Student Prompt Generation: Students author algorithmic challenges for their peers.
  • Real-time Complexity Benchmark: Code is benchmarked automatically by Space $O(1)$ and Time $O(N)$ efficiency.
  • Automated Grade Assignment: Professors review ranked submissions and assign grades with one click.
Result: 4.2x increase in peer-assisted problem solving & 100% real-time integrity verification.
